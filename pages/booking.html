Booking FAQ

Common questions about securing your spot.

Can I change my booking? expand_more
Yes, you can easily change your booking time by contacting us directly via WhatsApp or calling the gym at least 24 hours in advance.
Can I book multiple services? expand_more
Absolutely! Please mention the additional services you are interested in within the notes section of the booking form, and we will accommodate your request during the confirmation call.
Do I pay online? expand_more
No, booking a session does not require upfront online payment. Payment is typically handled at the gym during your visit, though some specific packages may require an advance deposit which we will discuss during confirmation.
How will my booking be confirmed? expand_more
We strive to confirm all bookings within 2-4 hours during our normal operating hours. Requests submitted late at night will be addressed first thing the following morning.
Can I book on behalf of someone else? expand_more
Yes, you can book on behalf of a friend or family member. Just provide their details in the contact fields and mention it in the notes so we know who to expect.
