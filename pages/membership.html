Frequently Asked Questions

Which plan is best for beginners? expand_more

Monthly is the most beginner-friendly option for most people because it balances structure, flexibility, and value. If you want extra guidance, you can add the optional personal trainer.

Can I upgrade later? expand_more

Yes. You can move from Daily or Weekly into a Monthly or longer plan whenever you're ready.

Can I add personal training? expand_more

Yes. Personal training is optional and costs an additional ₦10,000.

What happens after registration? expand_more

You can start training right away, meet a coach if you choose the PT add-on, and begin tracking your progress from your first session.

Does the 30-Visit Flex Pass expire? expand_more

No. Unlike monthly memberships, the 30-Visit Flex Pass is based on visits rather than calendar time. It remains active until you complete all 30 visits.

Who is the 30-Visit Flex Pass for? expand_more

It is ideal for travelers, busy professionals, and anyone whose schedule makes a monthly gym membership difficult.

How are my visits tracked? expand_more

Each gym visit is recorded as one signature/check-in. Your membership is completed when you reach your 30th visit.

Can I use the visits whenever I want? expand_more

Yes. The plan is designed for flexible schedules, so you can use your 30 visits without a monthly expiry.

How much is the 30-Visit Flex Pass? expand_more

The plan is ₦45,000, plus a ₦5,000 registration fee for first-time registration.
