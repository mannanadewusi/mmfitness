Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting your gear at MM Fitness.

Do you deliver?

Currently, we operate on an in-gym pickup model. You can enquire via WhatsApp, and once confirmed, your product will be ready for collection at the MM Fitness reception.

Can I reserve products?

Yes! Message us on WhatsApp to check availability, and we can reserve items for you to pick up during your next visit.

Can non-members purchase?

Absolutely. While we curate our essentials for our training community, our gym shop is open to everyone.

How do I enquire?
